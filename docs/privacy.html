VYLO / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026. This policy explains how the current VYLO mobile game handles information and locally stored game progress.

Developer release note: This policy reflects the current offline VYLO implementation. Review and update it before publishing a build that adds ads, analytics, Firebase, crash reporting, online leaderboards, multiplayer, cloud saves, authentication, in-app purchases, camera/photo access, location, or any other SDK/network data processing.
1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VYLO handles information when you use the VYLO mobile game.

2. Offline-First Game

VYLO's current core gameplay is designed to work offline. The game does not require an account, login, backend server, or network connection for its core gameplay.

3. Local Game Data

VYLO may store game progress locally on your device using application storage. This includes scores, best scores, gameplay statistics, earned Bolts, unlocked visual themes, selected theme, achievements, and game settings.

4. Scores and Best Scores

Scores from Classic, Endless, and Daily modes may be stored locally so the game can display your personal best results.

5. Gameplay Statistics

VYLO may locally store statistics such as runs played, total successful matches, and best combo to provide progress and statistics screens.

6. Bolts and Unlocks

Bolts earned through gameplay and locally unlocked themes are stored on the device. Bolts are an in-game progress value in the current version and are not real money, cryptocurrency, or a cash-equivalent asset.

7. Achievements

Achievement progress and unlocked achievement states are stored locally to show your gameplay accomplishments.

8. Themes

The selected visual theme and unlocked theme states may be stored locally so your chosen visual appearance remains available between sessions.

9. Daily Mode

The current Daily mode uses a date-based deterministic game sequence. It does not require an online leaderboard or remote account in the current implementation.

10. Settings

Sound and haptic preferences may be stored locally so VYLO can remember your gameplay settings.

11. Haptic Feedback

If enabled, VYLO may use standard device haptic feedback for gameplay interactions. This does not require VYLO to collect personal information.

12. Sound

The current implementation may use lightweight device/system sound feedback. It does not require microphone access.

13. Personal Information

The current core version of VYLO does not ask you to provide a name, phone number, postal address, account password, or other profile information.

14. Account Registration

The current version does not require account creation or sign-in for core gameplay.

15. Camera and Photos

The current VYLO implementation does not require camera or photo-library access.

16. Microphone

VYLO does not require microphone access for its current core gameplay.

17. Contacts

VYLO does not require access to your contacts for its current core gameplay.

18. Location

VYLO does not require precise or approximate device location for its current core gameplay.

19. Advertising

The current core implementation does not include advertising SDKs. If advertising is added later, this Privacy Policy and the applicable app-store disclosures must be reviewed and updated before that release.

20. Analytics and Behavioral Tracking

The current core implementation does not require third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs. Future introduction of analytics or tracking requires updated disclosures and any legally required consent.

21. Data Sharing and Sale

VYLO does not sell or rent the locally stored gameplay progress used by the current core application. The current implementation does not intentionally transmit this local gameplay progress to unrelated third parties.

22. Third-Party Services

Future versions may introduce services such as cloud saves, online leaderboards, authentication, multiplayer, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, purchases, or other network services. Relevant disclosures must be updated before such functionality is released.

23. Data Retention

Locally stored VYLO progress generally remains on your device until it is reset through the game's controls, application storage is cleared, the device is reset, or the game is uninstalled.

24. Resetting Progress

VYLO includes a local reset option that can delete stored scores, Bolts, theme unlocks, achievements, and other saved progress. Resetting progress cannot necessarily affect independent device backups controlled by your operating system or backup provider.

25. Device Backups

Depending on your operating-system and backup settings, the device or platform provider may independently back up application data. Those backups are governed by the provider's own policies and settings.

26. Security

VYLO is designed to keep its current core progress data local. However, no device, software, or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

27. Children's Privacy

VYLO is a general-audience arcade game. The current core version does not require users to create an online account or submit personal contact information. If future features collect personal information from children, additional protections and disclosures may be required.

28. International Users

The current core gameplay data is not intentionally transferred to a VYLO-operated remote server. Any independent device backup or future third-party online service may be governed by its own data-processing locations and policies.

29. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VYLO changes, new functionality is introduced, or legal and platform requirements change. The latest published version should be reviewed before each release.
